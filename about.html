About TechFest 2026

TechFest 2026 is the annual technical festival of our college. It provides students with opportunities to showcase innovation, creativity, teamwork, and technical skills.

The event brings together students, developers, and technology enthusiasts to learn, compete and create amazing solutions.

💡 Our Vision

To inspire students through technology, innovation, and healthy competition.

Encourage learning, collaboration, and practical experience through exciting events.
